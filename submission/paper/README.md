## Narrative (per AAAI AC analysis)

The headline is **calibrated deficit routing** (ablation +10.3%, the strongest component). Dual-signal diagnosis is framed as a *supporting* mechanism (ablation +1.3%), not the headline, to avoid the "core contribution not validated" rejection risk. Human eval (Q1 existence, 84.6% preference, p=4e-7) independently corroborates the automatic SCR result and is highlighted in abstract + discussion.

### Claims audit (2026-07-25)

Every significance claim was recomputed and several were **corrected downward** —
the earlier draft over-stated them:

| Claim as drafted | Recomputed | Action |
|---|---|---|
| "15/16 paired comparisons significant" | **11 of 12** MIDC-vs-baseline comparisons | corrected in abstract, intro, §4.2, §5 |
| MIDC vs best_of_n, hard_4 SCR: `p=0.050` | `−0.022`, CI `[−0.044,+0.000]`, **`p=0.056`** | now stated as *not* significant, and not claimed |
| "On FLUX.2 MIDC outperforms one-shot **and best-of-8** on both metrics" | vs one-shot 4/4 significant; **vs best-of-8 only 1/4** (6-ent DINO, p=0.002; others p=0.067/0.312/0.233) | abstract + §4.3 rewritten; the aggregate best-of-8 win is explicitly *not* claimed |
| "the gap **widens** as subjects increase" | −0.035 (6 ent) → −0.041 (8 ent), intervals overlap, interaction never tested | claim withdrawn; §4.3 says so explicitly |
| "MIDC … while *beating* best-of-8 on every metric" (compute ¶) | not supported | rewritten |

The FLUX.2 aggregate significance tests are new — the earlier draft reported only
the triggered-subset tests, which was the hole that produced the over-claim.
§4.5 now carries the load: MIDC's best-of-$N$ advantage is real but *concentrated*
on the rows it elects to correct.

A limitation paragraph on **SCR's dependence on its detector front end** was added
to §5, prompted by a manual inspection that found cases where the Grounding-DINO
subject count disagrees with a human reading in both directions. The paragraph
notes these errors apply identically to all methods (inflating variance, not
biasing comparisons) and that the human study reaches the same conclusion without
any detector.
